# Probably Weather product spine

**Status: DRAFT — AWAITING AL'S SIGNATURE**

Date drafted: 2026-07-14

A proposed capability belongs in Probably Weather only when it passes every criterion below:

1. It creates no new daily job for the user.
2. It is explainable in one glance.
3. It never weakens “Probably” honesty.
4. It never obscures Share.

## Ruled KILLs

- Radar
- 15-day forecasts
- Weather news feeds
- Collectibles
- Metric dashboards

These remain KILLs unless Al explicitly amends the signed product spine.
