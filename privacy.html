Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 May 2026

Probably Weather is a free weather application. Your privacy matters to us, and we've kept things as simple as possible.

What we collect

When you use Probably Weather, we request your device's location (latitude and longitude) to fetch weather data for where you are. If GPS is unavailable or blocked, the app may use an approximate IP-based location so it can still show a forecast. We do not store, log, or track your location on our servers.

Saved locations

If you save locations (like "Home" or "Work"), these are stored locally on your device only — in your browser's local storage or on the Android app's local data. We never see or access your saved places.

No accounts, no tracking

Probably Weather does not require registration or sign-in. We do not use cookies for tracking. We do not use analytics services. We do not serve advertisements. We do not collect personal information of any kind.

Third-party weather providers

Your location coordinates are sent to the following services to retrieve weather data:

Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) receives approximate latitude and longitude to return forecast data. It is necessary because it is one of our forecast sources. Privacy/terms: open-meteo.com/en/terms

WeatherAPI.com (weatherapi.com) receives approximate latitude and longitude to return forecast and condition-code data. It is necessary because it is one of our forecast sources. Privacy policy: weatherapi.com/privacy.aspx

MET Norway (Norwegian Meteorological Institute, api.met.no) receives approximate latitude and longitude to return forecast data. It is necessary because it is one of our forecast sources. Privacy policy: met.no privacy policy

Pirate Weather (api.pirateweather.net, API access via Apiable) receives approximate latitude and longitude to return forecast data. It is necessary because it is one of our forecast sources. API information: docs.pirateweather.net · Apiable privacy policy: apiable.io/terms/privacy-policy

Each of these services has their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them if you have concerns.

Location lookup services

Nominatim / OpenStreetMap (operated by the OpenStreetMap Foundation) receives your typed search query when you search for a place, and receives approximate latitude and longitude when the app converts coordinates into a readable place name. This is necessary for location search and place-name lookup. Privacy policy: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy · Nominatim usage policy: operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim

ipapi.co (Kloudend, Inc.) may receive your IP address when GPS is unavailable or denied, so the app can estimate a nearby city and still show a useful forecast. This fallback is only used when the app cannot get a device GPS location. Privacy policy: ipapi.co/privacy

Data stored on your device

The app stores your preferences (temperature units, wind units, language) and saved locations in local storage on your device. You can clear this data at any time through your browser or Android app settings.

Children's privacy

Probably Weather is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Since we don't collect email addresses, we can't notify you directly — but there's nothing to worry about because we don't collect your data in the first place.
