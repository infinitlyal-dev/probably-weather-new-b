        2. 2 Scroll, tap "Add to Home Screen"
        3. 3 Tap × above to close these instructions

        Probably Weather will appear on your home screen — tap the icon to open the app.

        Don't see "Add to Home Screen"? Tap "Edit Actions" at the bottom of the Share menu and turn it on.
