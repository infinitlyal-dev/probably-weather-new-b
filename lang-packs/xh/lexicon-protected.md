## Attestation discipline (the isiXhosa-specific rule)
The biggest isiXhosa risk in this app is not calque but **unattested tokens** — plausible-looking
words the model produced that no native uses. The protected move: when unsure, choose the PLAIN
attested word over the ornate unconfirmed one, and tag LOW. Watch word boundaries — do not fuse
morphemes (`lwengqele olukwenza`, not `lwengqeleolukwenza`).

**Register anchor:** braai, i-Toyota, sunscreen, place names, "lekker" stay as loans — natural
urban isiXhosa. The error is a mistranslated/unattested content word, never a real loan.
