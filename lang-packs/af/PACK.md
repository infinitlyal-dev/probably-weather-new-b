# Afrikaans language pack — voice reference (Al's register)

**Language:** Afrikaans (South African colloquial — Al's own voice).
**Status:** AF is **fully filled** — 989 confirmed strings, **0 debt**. This pack is therefore a
**VOICE REFERENCE**, not a drafting target. Its job: capture Al's Afrikaans register so future
AF drafts (new features, reroll waves) start from his voice, and so zu/xh/st drafters can read the
AF line as the sharpest native-SA signal for a string's intent.

**Al is the native source.** There is no web harvest for AF — the corpus IS the native's own
wording. Every AF string in the banks was written or ruled by Al (see commits `95fa5e8` 52-line
wording rulings, `9803b8c` binnestebuite fix, `2fe4972` gap-fill, and `7e008bb` meme-batch AF).

## Al's voice (the register other languages should echo in intent)
- **Warm, dry, self-aware, code-switched.** A Boland/Strand voice, not textbook Afrikaans.
- **SA colloquial markers he uses:** "Jinne", "hey", "nè", "boet", "lekker", "kuier", "windgat",
  "jou bek te hou" (shut up — affectionate), "tjailatyd", "hoofseun sindroom", "afskeep werk".
- **Keeps loans naturally:** braai, Weber, bakkie, Eskom, beurtkrag, brand names, place names,
  English tech words — mid-sentence, unforced.
- **Idiom over literal:** "Die Dokter doen sy oggendrondtes" (the Cape Doctor / SE wind), "die
  tafeldoek is gedek" (Table Mountain cloth) — imagery localised, not translated.
- **Punchy structure:** short, two-beat jokes; the second sentence lands the punch.

## Errors Al corrected (see errors-observed.md)
Half-English drafts ("Sky's playing kat-en-muis"), textbook stiffness, literal calques of English
puns — Al rewrites these into full, idiomatic, colloquial AF. The pattern: make it MORE South
African, never more formal.

## Use for zu/xh/st drafting
When a zu/xh/st draft is uncertain, read the AF line: it shows how a native SA speaker localised
the JOKE (not the words). Echo the intent and register, conceive fresh in the target language —
never translate the AF word-for-word.
