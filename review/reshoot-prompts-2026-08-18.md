## The two rulings these are written under

**Countrywide, not Cape Town.** Al, 2026-08-18: *"dont just make it Helderberg
suburban, Johburg, Durban etc. The rest of the country needs to be part of it to
otherwise it just starts feeling like a CT app and not countrywide."* Weighted to
where each weather signature actually lives: Highveld thunderstorms in Gauteng,
subtropical downpours in KZN, the wind belt across the Eastern and Western Cape.

Spread: Gauteng 4 · KZN 4 · Western Cape 3 · Eastern Cape 2 · Free State 1 ·
Northern Cape 1.

**Wind and storm live in what is STILL STANDING.** Every cut frame had one prop
knocked flat and centred — a chair, a washtub, a gazebo. That is explaining the
joke. These show things straining and holding: washing pulled horizontal, a palm
raked over, a shade cloth bellying, dust ribboning through a security light.

## The tail appended to every prompt

> Documentary photograph, vertical 9:16, 35mm lens, available light only, no
> people. Real weathered surfaces — damp patches, rust, hairline cracks,
> sun-faded paint, marks where things have been leaned. No CGI or 3D-render
> look, no plastic sheen on any surface, no green colour cast on cream or white
> walls. Nothing blown over or lying broken. Keep the lower third of the frame
> visually quiet and uncluttered.

That last line is new and structural: the home screen now writes the joke across
the bottom of the picture over a dark scrim, so anything important down there is
buried. The composition brief changed when the hero did.
