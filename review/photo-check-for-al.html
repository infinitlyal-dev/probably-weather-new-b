Probably Weather — the photo check

24 Sept. Two questions: did the last few days of cutting and moving break anything, and how many photos does D's joke cover, in all five languages. This was a check, not a fix: no photo and no line was changed, nothing was pushed, and the live app is still ec7ae52. Full detail: review/eval/EVAL.md, section 4c.

1. Is the rotation whole?

Yes. Nothing is missing and nothing is broken. There is one new small thing for you to decide (below).

  • I ran the app's own photo picker over every kind of weather, every week of the 4-week cycle, every time of day and every weekday: 1 568 spots. Every spot gets a photo, and all 294 photo files open. No gaps, no broken files.
  • To be sure the check matches the real app, I opened the app 16 times at chosen days and times: it showed exactly the photo the check said, 16 out of 16.
  • No photo shows in two kinds of weather in the same week.
  • Every photo gets a line in every place, every month and every language — 5.9 million combinations checked. The line is never blank. It is never English in Afrikaans, isiZulu, isiXhosa or Sesotho, except the two safety lines you chose to keep in English known.
  • No line talks about weather its folder doesn't have. (49 lines use a weather word; I read each one and they all fit — list below.)
  • Nothing needed fixing in the code.
