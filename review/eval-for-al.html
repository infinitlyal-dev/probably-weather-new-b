What I changed from the sketch, and why (one line each)

  • The joke moves up under "Probably …" when your crop anchor says the subject is low and the top of the photo is clear — what TV subtitles do when the bottom of the picture matters. It moves on 65 photos and takes the joke off the subject on 45 of the 73; the other 28 keep it, because their subject sits in the lower middle, where a risen joke would land on it; and it never moves the joke onto a subject that was clear. It can't know what a joke is about: the pale photo's joke is about the dog at the bottom while your anchor marks the family, so there the joke stays on the dog.
  • The nav stays (Home, Weekly, Search, Settings) on the photo's dark foot, at reading size; Share left it for a button on the photo.
  • Share sends a picture of the screen — photo, number, joke and facts, with probablyweather.co.za at the foot — where the phone can share pictures; where it can't, it sends today's link card.
  • The desktop keeps today's polaroid. The photos are portrait: filling a computer screen would crop away most of each one, and the desktop already shows the big number and the week without a tap.
  • A wind warning still shows as the orange bar across the top; the number and everything under it step down below it.

Costs: effort large — about 1,000 lines on this branch (the layout, the panel, the Share picture); speed: nothing extra on today's Home, and D's own code is 9 KB; rulings: the five above. Everything on D is at reading size (16 px or more; the joke's handwriting never under 19 px), and it fits your screen and all 80 test screens down to the smallest.
