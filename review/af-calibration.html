Afrikaans: 40 lines, your ear

These are 40 Afrikaans lines from the app, next to the English they come from. None of them is one you have ruled on before. For each one, say whether the Afrikaans is right: GOOD — it says what the English says, in natural Afrikaans. DRIFT BUT FINE — it changes something on purpose and keeps the joke; you would ship it. WRONG — it says something else, loses the joke, or reads wrong; you would not ship it. Some of these passed the machine check and some did not. They are mixed and not marked, so your answers show which checker to trust. There is no trick and no quota. A note is welcome where you know the better Afrikaans. Choices save in this browser.
