Swept: all 994 live hero lines in `review/set-001-lines-bespoke-final.json` (English) and their Afrikaans in `assets/hero-lines-af.js`,
against a list of ~250 South African towns, suburbs, regions, provinces, mountains, passes, roads (N/R numbers) and demonyms
(Capetonians, Kapenaars, Joburg, Highveld …). Not counted: "South Africa" (the whole app is South African), the Southern Cross,
overseas names in a joke (NZ, Antarctica, Silent Hill), the Afrikaans word *strand* (beach), "the mountain" / "the city" with no name.

**47 lines on 39 photographs.** The rule being applied (Maat's file header, Al, 7 Oct): no town or landmark names,
because the photograph rotates by weekday and hour, not by the user's town.

**One fact for the ruling:** all but one of these lines already carry a *place gate* from Al's 23 Sept ruling
(`review/place-lines-ruled.json`): the line is only shown to someone whose location is inside the named region — a Joburg line
never reaches Strand. What the gate cannot fix is the photograph: the line names a place the picture may not show
(e.g. "Table Mountain is right behind her"). If the rewrites are used, the gate on those lines can come off and the line
shows to everyone who gets that photograph — recommended, since nothing in the rewrite is regional any more.
The only ungated line is #26 "Even the Capetonians are admitting it's cold." — it shows nationwide today.

Proposals keep each joke's turn and swap only the name (the veld, the mountain, the city, the highway, this town).
The proposed Afrikaans went through lang-check (af): all pass.
zu/xh/st for any line Al takes go to the native backlog as usual.

**My picks** (pre-marked so Al only changes what he disagrees with): use every proposal in A; keep B (a wind's name is not a
town, and both lines stay gated to the Western Cape); use both Afrikaans fixes in C.
