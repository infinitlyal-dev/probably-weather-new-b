## 2. "Might rain" from the day's 50 % now reads the next 8 hours, and fog stands

The phone used to say "Might rain." all day whenever the whole day's rain chance was 50 % or more, even when that rain was already over or was 12 hours away: rain actually followed within 12 hours only 44 % of the time. Now it says so only when the next 4 hours are dry and one of the 4 hours after that is 50 % or more. Rain followed that within 8 hours 81 % of the time. I picked 8 hours because it is "the next few hours" and it held up best on the airports. A fog call from the server now stands over it. Across six airports: 716 false "might rain" hours gone (phone might-rain hours 2,266 → 1,505); 76 fog calls no longer overwritten; the cost is 28 hours where it did rain and the hero now shows nothing wet (rain missed 21.4 % → 24.9 % of 800 rain hours), spread over 12 airport-days, against 86 days better. The "Rain later" wording in the stats row follows the same rule. Server, rain-now and wind unchanged. Replay case: Cape Town 16 Sept 08:00 — a 69.5 % day whose rain was over, "Might rain." all morning under scattered cloud. Tables: `review/accuracy/results/compare-later-before-vs-later-after.md`.
