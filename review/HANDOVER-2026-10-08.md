## Traps learned this run

- In the `api/weather.js` handler, `windDecision` is defined AFTER `windSourcesAt25`; anything reading it must go after `const windDecision = windLine(windNow);` (two TDZ breaks this run, both caught by the suite).
- The harness replay (`review/accuracy/lib/replay.mjs`) has no radar, no WeatherAPI wording, no station override, no v5–v7 wind rules; radar/thunder evidence comes from the live recorder (`.jsonl` git-ignored, at `C:\Users\27741\OneDrive\Desktop\Probably weather new\probably-weather-new-c\review\accuracy\live`).
- SA METARs never report high cloud and half are CAVOK: cloud scoring can count missed grey skies, not cirrus.
- Python heredoc replacements mangle `\n` and `\'` in JS — use the Edit tool for those.
