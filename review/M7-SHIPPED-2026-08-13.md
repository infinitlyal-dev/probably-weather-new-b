# M7 — the anchors Al ruled, shipped

Al ruled 167 images on 2026-08-13 in `review/crop-anchor-tool.html`, one at a
time, dragging the crop band onto the subject. His words, recorded because they
set what this is: *"I am not convinced this will work in the long run. I do
believe we may need to do quite a couple of them over or replace them but for
now it will be easier for me to judge them on my phone for the next day or two
when they are live."*

So this ships to be judged on a real phone, not as a final answer.

## The provenance chain

2. `review/set-001-crop-offsets.json` — the same ruling in the authoring shape

## What was verified before shipping

- **The anchors reach the pixel.** `scripts/verify-crop-anchors-live.mjs` boots
  the built app across eight condition/time combinations and asserts, for
  whatever photograph the picker lands on, that the painted
  `background-position` equals that image's table value — or 78% when it has no
  entry. Result: 2 loads landed on ruled images and painted 33% and 23%, 6
  landed on un-ruled ones and painted 78%. Both halves matter: an
  everything-anchored pass would hide a constant being applied to every image.
- **The reroll guard held** on all 167 — every hash still matches the bytes on
  disk, so no ruling has drifted onto a different photograph.
- **Two bugs fixed on the way in.** The wiring script read `entry.cropY` while
  the anchor files use `anchorY` (renamed at Al's "percentages, not pixels"
  ruling) — it would have skipped all 167 silently and exited clean. And the
  `hero-crop` test asserting "the table is empty ON PURPOSE" was the deliberate
  gate on shipping offsets; it now guards the other direction — every shipped
  value must be one Al actually set.

## Known and accepted

- Roughly a third of the earlier FIXABLE set would probably survive at the
  default now (the M9 flex hero shows 41% more of each source than the box the
  2026-08-09 ruling was made against). Anchoring them anyway is harmless — an
  unnecessary anchor still frames the subject — and Al's live pass is the right
  instrument for finding the ones that are wrong.
- 127 images carry no anchor and paint the 78% default.
